A few useful details.

When can I download Photo Pro?

Photo Pro is being prepared for the Mac App Store. The download link and pricing will appear here when it is released.

Does Photo Pro need an account?

The current app has no account system. You edit files locally on your Mac.

Where are my documents saved?

You choose where to save your documents and exports. Settings, presets and recovery data are kept locally by the app.
